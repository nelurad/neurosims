Select a clearance mechanism to visualize how neurotransmitters are cleared from the synaptic cleft in real-time.

1. Reuptake via Transporters (DAT / SERT)
Monoamine neurotransmitters like Dopamine (DA), Serotonin (5-HT), and Norepinephrine (NE) are pumped directly back into the presynaptic terminal via specialized symporters (DAT, SERT, NET). Once inside, they are repackaged into synaptic vesicles by VMAT2 for reuse.
