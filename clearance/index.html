Synaptic Clearance Mechanisms

After neurotransmitters cross the synaptic cleft, they must be removed immediately to turn off the signal and prepare for the next message. Choose a mechanism below to see how different synapses clear the cleft.

0.20 s

Enzymatic Breakdown (Acetylcholinesterase)

Enzymes sitting directly in the cleft snap acetylcholine into choline and acetate within 1–2 milliseconds.

Key location: Neuromuscular junction & autonomic synapses.

Simplified hand-drawn model. Clearance rates and times are scaled for visual clarity. In living tissue, enzymatic breakdown occurs in less than 2 milliseconds, whereas presynaptic reuptake and diffusion take tens of milliseconds.
